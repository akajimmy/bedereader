# BeDeReader - privacy

BeDeReader collects no data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no account of its own, and its developer receives nothing from it.

The app talks only to the places you give it: your Komga server, and shared folders on your network. What it reads there and what it writes there (your reading progress, your edits) stays between you and them.

On iPhone and iPad it asks for Local Network access, to reach a server or a shared folder at home; it asks for nothing else.

Your Komga API key (or, if your server won't issue one, your email and password) and the logins of shared folders are stored on the device in the Keychain. Your server's address is stored in the app's own settings. Removing the app removes them.

A hidden diagnostic option can send the app's own log to a server you set up; it is off unless you turn it on.
